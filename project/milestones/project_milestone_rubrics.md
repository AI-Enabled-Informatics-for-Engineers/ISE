# Semester Project — Milestones, Deliverables, and Rubrics  
**Course:** AI-Enabled Informatics for Engineers  
**Format:** GitHub-first · decision-driven · milestone-based  
**Project Goal:**  
Build an **AI-enabled informatics system** that improves a **real decision workflow**  
—not just a model, but a **reliable decision system**.

---

# Project Philosophy

This project mirrors **real AI engineering practice**:

- Decisions matter more than models  
- Data reliability matters more than accuracy alone  
- Evaluation must reflect **cost, risk, and impact**  
- Systems must be **auditable, secure, and human-centered**

Each milestone represents a **real phase of an AI system lifecycle**.

# M0 — Domain Shortlist *(Week 1 · Ungraded)*

## Goal
Identify **three realistic domains** and a **decision in each** before committing.

## Deliverables (Canvas)
- 3 domains  
- 1 decision statement per domain  
*(6–12 bullets total)*

## Checklist
- Each decision is an **action**, not just “predict X”
- At least **one domain uses a public dataset**
- You can explain **why the domain matters**

## Helpful Resources
- Kaggle datasets → https://www.kaggle.com/datasets

---

# M1 — Problem Definition *(Week 2 · 4 pts)*

## Goal
Commit to a **single decision problem** with measurable success.

## Deliverables
- Domain + stakeholder (1–2 sentences)  
- Decision statement (1 sentence)  
- Dataset plan (link + what/why/limits)  
- Success criteria (2–3 measurable bullets)

## Rubric

| Category | Points | Meets Expectations | Exceeds Expectations |
|----------|--------|-------------------|---------------------|
| Decision clarity | 1 | Mostly clear | Precise, actionable, stakeholder-aware |
| Dataset plan | 1 | Identified | Justified, realistic, ethical limits noted |
| Success criteria | 1 | Some metrics | Measurable + tied to impact/cost |
| Communication | 1 | Readable | Professional and concise |

---

# M2 — Pipeline, Schema, Evaluation *(Week 4 · 4 pts)*

## Goal
Design the **informatics backbone** of the system.

## Deliverables
- Pipeline diagram *(ingest → transform → store → serve)*  
- Data schema *(tables, fields, types)*  
- Evaluation plan aligned to **decision cost**

## Rubric

| Category | Points | Meets | Exceeds |
|----------|--------|-------|---------|
| Pipeline design | 1 | Basic | Realistic + risks identified |
| Schema quality | 1 | Functional | Well-structured + query-ready |
| Evaluation alignment | 1 | Uses metrics | Cost-aligned + decision-aware |
| Clarity | 1 | Readable | Professional diagram + structure |

## Helpful Resources
- Prefect → https://docs.prefect.io/  
- NIST AI RMF → https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework  

---

# M3 — MVP Demo *(Week 6 · 4 pts)*

## Goal
Demonstrate a **working end-to-end decision system**.

## Deliverables
- Runnable notebook or repo  
- 2–4 minute walkthrough video **or** annotated notebook  
- Short **operator playbook**

## Rubric

| Category | Points | Meets | Exceeds |
|----------|--------|-------|---------|
| Working system | 1.5 | Runs | Reliable + repeatable |
| Decision usefulness | 1 | Linked to decision | Clear threshold + rationale |
| Walkthrough clarity | 0.75 | Basic | Structured + insightful |
| Code/readability | 0.75 | Readable | Clean + well-organized |

---

# M4 — Architecture Packet *(Week 8 · 4 pts · Midterm)*

## Goal
Explain **real-world system operation and risk**.

## Deliverables
- Architecture diagram  
- Risk list *(5–10 items)*  
- Mitigation list *(mapped to risks)*

## Rubric

| Category | Points | Meets | Exceeds |
|----------|--------|-------|---------|
| Architecture clarity | 1.5 | Major parts shown | Complete + realistic |
| Risk analysis | 1.25 | Some risks | Prioritized + meaningful |
| Mitigations | 1.25 | Generic | Concrete + trade-off aware |

---

# M5 — Advanced Capability *(Week 10 · 4 pts)*

## Goal
Add **trust or usefulness beyond MVP**.

Examples:

- RAG grounding + citations  
- Calibration & thresholds  
- Monitoring dashboards  
- Retrieval evaluation  

## Deliverables
- Notebook with working feature  
- 1-page report *(impact + evidence)*

## Rubric

| Category | Points | Meets | Exceeds |
|----------|--------|-------|---------|
| Technical addition | 1.5 | Partial | Fully working + valuable |
| Evaluation evidence | 1.5 | Some | Strong + metric-driven |
| Communication | 1 | Clear | Concise + compelling |

---

# M6 — Trust & Security Package *(Week 13 · 4 pts)*

## Goal
Demonstrate **responsible AI and cybersecurity readiness**.

## Deliverables

- Logging/audit plan  
- Responsible-AI notes *(harms, oversight, transparency)*

## Rubric

| Category | Points | Meets | Exceeds |
|----------|--------|-------|---------|
| Threat modeling | 1.5 | Basic | Realistic + structured |
| Mitigation & audit | 1.5 | Some | Fully mapped + auditable |
| Responsible AI | 1 | Mentioned | Concrete harm-reduction steps |

# M7 — Final Delivery *(Week 14 · 6 pts)*

## Goal
Deliver a **complete, runnable, decision-impacting informatics system**.

## Deliverables

- Final report *(3–6 pages)*  
- Runnable artifact + README  

## Rubric

| Category | Points |
|----------|--------|
| Decision clarity | 1 |
| System quality | 2 |
| Evaluation & evidence | 1.5 |

# Submission Principles

All milestones must:

- Be **decision-driven**
- Be **reproducible**
- Include **clear assumptions**
- Reflect **real-world engineering trade-offs**

---

# Final Thoughts

This project is **not about building the most complex AI**.

It is about building the **most meaningful, reliable, and responsible decision system**.

That distinction is the essence of **AI-enabled informatics**.
